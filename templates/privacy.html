Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Spendly. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, submit expense data, or communicate with us. This may include:

  • Personal information such as name, email address, and password
  • Financial data including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Usage data such as features accessed, buttons clicked, and time spent in the app
  • Device information including IP address, browser type, and operating system

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our service, including:

  • To process and store your expense data securely
  • To provide personalized insights and spending reports
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, and promotional offers
  • To improve our app's functionality and user experience
  • To prevent fraud, ensure security, and comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Your data is:

  • Stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption
  • Protected by regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Backed up regularly to prevent data loss
  • Accessed only by authorized personnel who need it to perform their duties

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate our business (subject to confidentiality agreements)
  • When required by law, such as to respond to subpoenas or legal requests
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, or that of others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it for processing your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention policies.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
